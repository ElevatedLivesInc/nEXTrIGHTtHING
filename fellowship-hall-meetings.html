Mission

Why the doors stay open

“The Fellowship Foundation exists to provide a facility for mutually supportive association among its members — where better living, good citizenship, charity, justice, and brotherly love may be cultivated in a safe, alcohol- and drug-free environment.”

Open & accessible. All meetings are considered open (visitors welcome, non-specific) unless otherwise noted, and all meetings are wheelchair accessible.
Newcomers welcome. Members keep the doors open so newcomers can come in and twelve-step meetings can be safely held.
A 501(c)(3) charity. The Fellowship Foundation owns and operates the Hall as a nonprofit charitable organization.
