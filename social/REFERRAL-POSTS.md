# "Who sent you?" — launch posts

Pair every one of these with `NRT-yardsale-who-sent-you.png`.
Destination is always `nextrighthing.com/yard-sale` — never a tracking link.
The referrer is a name the friend types on the form. That is the whole design.

---

## THE LINE THAT MUST NOT DRIFT

Referrals earn **recognition**, never entries, never odds, never a discount.

The moment a post says "get an extra chance" or "more entries," you've created
consideration — and Utah has no charitable-raffle exemption to fall back on.
Say **"thanked by name."** Never **"extra chances."** If someone in the comments
asks whether referring helps them win, the answer is: *"Nope — it just gets you
thanked out loud. Odds are the same for everybody."*

---

## Facebook / Instagram — main post

> There's a new question on the entry form: **"Who sent you?"**
>
> Here's why. Last year the people who actually filled that driveway weren't
> us — they were the ones who texted three friends and said *you need to come
> see this.*
>
> So this year we're keeping track. Send the link to one person. When they
> enter — free, eleven seconds, no purchase — they type your name in the box.
>
> Whoever brings the most people gets thanked **by name**, out loud, Monday
> afternoon at the sale.
>
> That's the whole prize. No extra entries, no discount, no odds changed —
> everybody's chances stay exactly the same. Just your name said out loud in
> front of people, for doing the thing nobody thinks counts.
>
> 2nd Annual Yard Sale Fundraiser · Sept 4–7 · 8 AM daily
> 📍 7437 S Ramanee Dr, Midvale
> 🔗 nextrighthing.com/yard-sale
>
> Every dollar goes to The Right Thing in Recovery, a 501(c)(3).

## Instagram story — 3 frames

1. **"New question on the entry form."** → sticker: *Who sent you?*
2. **"Send it to one person. They type your name."** → link sticker
3. **"Most people brought gets thanked by name at the sale."** → *No extra
   entries. Odds stay the same. That's on purpose.*

> in the who-sent-you box. It doesn't get you anything except your name said
