Join Us

You're invited to join us for an exciting evening of community, celebration, and giving back at The Next Right Thing in Recovery's Flannel to Formal Masquerade Fundraiser!

We're opening our doors for a special open house where you can meet our team, learn more about our mission, and help support the work we do to make a difference in the lives of individuals and families impacted by addiction.

Whether you come dressed in flannel, formal attire, or your favorite masquerade style, we would love to have you there!

Your presence and support help us continue our mission of providing hope, recovery, and the opportunity for a better future. This is a wonderful chance to connect with our community, celebrate second chances, and support a cause that truly matters.

Come for the fun, stay for the mission, and help us make a difference!

With gratitude,
The Next Right Thing in Recovery Team

Good to know

Questions

Can I pay at the door?

Yes. Reserving online just lets the kitchen plan dinner. You can pay online (when available) or at the door.

Is my sponsorship or ticket tax-deductible?

Contributions benefit The Right Thing in Recovery, a 501(c)(3) nonprofit organization (EIN 93-4316358), and are tax-deductible to the extent allowed by law. If you receive tickets, dinner, or other benefits in return, your receipt will show their value, and only the amount above that value is deductible. Please consult your tax advisor.

What should I wear?

Flannel, formal, masquerade — or all three. Masks encouraged, never required.

Photos & privacy

We love a good photo booth, and we protect the people we serve. Please don't photograph, post, or tag anyone without asking first. Our staff will never share images of people in our care without their written consent.

I want to help but can't attend

Become a sponsor, donate an item, or enter the free drawing — all from this page. Questions? Contact or call 801-816-4977.
