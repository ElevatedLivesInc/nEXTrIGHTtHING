Join Us

You're invited to join us for an exciting evening of community, celebration, and giving back at The Next Right Thing in Recovery's Flannel to Formal Masquerade Fundraiser!

We're opening our doors for a special open house where you can meet our team, learn more about our mission, and help support the work we do to make a difference in the lives of individuals and families impacted by addiction.

Whether you come dressed in flannel, formal attire, or your favorite masquerade style, we would love to have you there!

Your presence and support help us continue our mission of providing hope, recovery, and the opportunity for a better future. This is a wonderful chance to connect with our community, celebrate second chances, and support a cause that truly matters.

Come for the fun, stay for the mission, and help us make a difference!

With gratitude,
The Next Right Thing in Recovery Team

The Starfish Wall

Light a starfish

$5 lights one starfish. $20 lights five. Each starfish carries your name or a few words of hope, and every one goes up on our wall the night of the event.

A child walked a beach covered in stranded starfish, throwing them back one at a time. Someone told her she could never make a difference. She threw one more and said, “It made a difference to that one.”

This is a gift, not a ticket: there are no prizes and no drawing.

Good to know

Questions

Can I pay at the door?

Yes. Reserving online just lets the kitchen plan dinner. You can pay online (when available) or at the door.

Is my sponsorship or ticket tax-deductible?

Contributions benefit The Right Thing in Recovery. Every gift gets a receipt, and if you receive tickets, dinner, or other benefits in return, your receipt will show their value. For questions about deductibility, please consult your tax advisor.

What should I wear?

Flannel, formal, masquerade — or all three. Masks encouraged, never required.

Photos & privacy

We love a good photo booth, and we protect the people we serve. Please don't photograph, post, or tag anyone without asking first. Our staff will never share images of people in our care without their written consent. Every ticket, sponsorship and entry form asks for your photo choice, and you can tell us or change it at check-in.

I want to help but can't attend

Become a sponsor, donate an item, bid in the silent auction, or light a starfish — all from this page. Questions? Contact or call 801-816-4977.
