# Day One launch plan — first sales with targeted precision

## The honest baseline
A brand-new Fiverr account has no reviews, no level and almost no search placement. **Day-one sales come from buyers you bring, not from Fiverr search.** Search placement comes later, earned by conversion rate, response time, on-time delivery and reviews (Fiverr lists these as level criteria). So the plan has two lanes:

| Lane | Goal | Source of buyers |
|---|---|---|
| **A. Warm (day 1–3)** | 3–6 real orders → first reviews | The 6 named prospects + personal network |
| **B. Cold (day 1–30)** | Fiverr search + Briefs | Keyword gig, fast replies, specific audits |

Lane A is what makes Lane B work. A gig with 3 genuine 5-star reviews converts very differently from a gig with zero.

**What's proven vs. folklore**
- Proven / stated by Fiverr: response time is displayed on gigs; level is based on orders, earnings, on-time delivery, response rate, ratings; gig video is encouraged; buyers can filter by seller online status.
- Proven by pricing research: three-tier pricing pulls buyers to the middle tier.
- Folklore (unverified, don't plan around it): the "new gig boost", "log in every hour to rank", "share your gig 100 times". Ignore.

## Hard rules (account-killers)
- **No fake orders, no friends ordering to leave reviews, no review swaps.** Fiverr detects and bans for this. A warm prospect who genuinely needs and pays for the work is a real order. That's lane A.
- Warm buyers ordering through Fiverr is fine, but you pay the 20% fee. **Use Fiverr for 2–3 warm clients to seed reviews, then bill the rest of the warm list directly** (Stripe/Square invoice, same SOP). Fiverr reviews are an asset; Fiverr fees on your own network are a cost.

### Days 2–7
- Deliver the warm orders **early** (aim for day 5 on a 7-day order). Early delivery + a specific delivery message is what earns 5-star reviews.
- Day 3: add the gig video.
- Day 4: clone Gig 1 into the Local SEO category (Gig 2).
- Every day: Briefs check at noon, sub-1-hour replies.

### Days 8–30
- After 3 reviews: publish the niche gig (sober living / recovery housing) — the moat.
- Turn on Fiverr **Promoted Gigs** if your account becomes eligible; cap the daily budget at $5–10 and keep it only if the cost per order is under $60.
- Weekly: look at gig analytics (impressions → clicks → orders). If clicks are low, change the cover image. If orders are low, change the first two lines of the description.

## Targeting — who buys $300 fastest
Score every prospect on three things (5 points each; 10+ = contact today):
1. **Visible gap** — no site, or a listing missing categories/services/photos (you can show it in a screenshot)
2. **Revenue per customer** — one new customer covers $300 (trades, home services, med-spa, auto, legal, dental, sober living, landscaping)
3. **Owner answers their own phone** — a decision-maker, not a committee

Avoid on Fiverr: restaurants with thin margins, multi-location chains, anyone asking for "guaranteed #1".

## Day-one success metric
Not "first sale." **3 qualified conversations and 1 order (warm or cold) by 9pm.** Then 3 reviews by day 10.
