# Message scripts — copy into Fiverr "Quick Responses"

Fiverr rules that shape every script below (read once, obey always):
- **No contact info, links or payment outside Fiverr** before or during an order. Don't type emails, phone numbers, "WhatsApp", or outside URLs in chat. Use Fiverr's own preview/delivery tools. The demo link goes in the gig gallery or the delivery, not the chat.
- **Never ask for or reward reviews** ("5 stars please", discounts for reviews). You may say a review helps a small team. That's it.
- Respond to every first message within 1 hour, because Fiverr shows response time on the gig.

Placeholders: `{first}`, `{business}`, `{city}`, `{gap}`.

---

## 1. Welcome / auto-reply (Fiverr setting → Welcome message)
> Hi! Thanks for reaching out to Rein N' Solutions. We're a two-person team in Salt Lake City and we reply within the hour, 7am–9pm MT.
> To point you to the right package fast, tell us: (1) your business + city, (2) do you have a website today, (3) is your Google Business Profile set up?

## 2. Quick response: "QUALIFY" (after they answer)
> Thanks, {first}. Based on what you said, here's the honest read:
> • Website: {have/none/needs rebuild}
> • Google Profile: {missing/incomplete/solid}
> The **Standard ($300)** package fixes both and adds the lead dashboard. If your website is already strong, **Basic ($150)** covers just the Google side. Want me to send a custom offer for either?

## 3. Quick response: "AUDIT" — the conversion closer
Do a 5-minute look at their Google listing *before* replying. Specific beats general every time.
> I took a quick look at {business} on Google. Three things I'd fix first:
> 1. {e.g. primary category is "Contractor" — "Handyman" matches what people search}
> 2. {e.g. no services listed, so you don't show for "gutter cleaning {city}"}
> 3. {e.g. 4 photos, none from the last year}
> All three are in the Standard package, plus the website and dashboard. I can start today and have a preview to you in 5 days.

## 4. Custom offer note (attach to offer)
> Website + Google Profile + Mission Control Lite for {business}. Preview in 5 days, launch by day 7, 2 revision rounds. After you order, Fiverr will show a 10-minute questionnaire. That's everything we need to start.

## 5. Objection: "Too expensive / I saw $50 gigs"
> Totally fair. The $50 gigs usually cover one piece: a template site *or* a listing tweak. This is the site, the Google profile and a dashboard that shows whether it's working, done by people who answer within an hour. If budget is tight, Basic ($150) fixes the Google side first, which is usually where the calls come from.

## 6. Objection: "Can you guarantee results / ranking?"
> No honest person can promise a rank. Google ranks local results on relevance, distance and prominence. We make relevance and prominence as strong as they can be: complete profile, right categories, real photos, a fast site and a review link you can share. Then the dashboard shows you the calls, so you're not guessing.

## 7. Order kickoff (send right after requirements are submitted)
> Got everything, {first}. Here's the schedule:
> • Day 1–2: Google Profile cleanup (add us as Manager. Steps: Google Business Profile → ⋮ → Business Profile settings → People and access → Add → our Fiverr-linked email, role "Manager")
> • Day 3–5: Website built. You get a preview on day 5
> • Day 6–7: Your revisions, launch, Mission Control handoff
> You'll hear from us at each step. Nothing for you to do until the preview.

## 8. Preview ready (day 5)
> Your preview is ready, uploaded here as a delivery draft with screenshots of every page. Reply with changes in one message if you can (a numbered list is perfect). Revisions are included.

## 9. Delivery message
> {business} is live 🎉
> ✔ Website: {domain}
> ✔ Google Profile: {list of 5–8 specific changes made}
> ✔ Mission Control Lite: your private link + 2-minute how-to video are in the attached PDF
> What to do this week: share your review link (in the PDF) with 5 happy customers.
> If anything looks off, message me. I'll fix it.

## 10. Post-delivery (day 3 after completion)
> Hi {first}, checking in. Any calls or form leads yet? Your dashboard should be showing them. If anything's confusing, I'll record a quick walkthrough.
> (A review on Fiverr helps a small team a lot, only if you're happy with the work.)

## 11. Upsell (day 21, Standard buyers only)
> Your first three weeks of numbers: {views} profile views, {calls} calls, {leads} form leads. The thing that moves these next is fresh activity: weekly Google posts + replying to every review. Premium-style upkeep is a $300/mo custom offer if you want us to just handle it.
