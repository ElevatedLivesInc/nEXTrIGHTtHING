## FAQ (add all)
1. **Do you need access to my Google Business Profile?** Yes — you add us as a Manager (not Owner) from your profile settings. We send a 3-step guide. You can remove us any time.
2. **Don't have a Google Business Profile yet?** We create it with you. Google verifies by video, phone or postcard on its own timeline; the website launches either way.
3. **Do I own the website?** Yes. It's yours, on your domain, and we hand over the files.
4. **What does hosting cost?** We host on Cloudflare Pages; for a small business site that is typically $0/month. Your domain is ~$10–20/year from any registrar.
5. **What is Mission Control Lite?** A private page only you can open, showing your leads from the website form, response times, and the Google numbers we enter weekly (Premium) or you enter in 1 minute (Standard). It is not a CRM; it is the scoreboard.
6. **Can you guarantee #1 on Google?** No one honest can. We guarantee a complete, policy-compliant profile and a fast site, which are the parts Google says you control.
7. **Do you write the text?** Yes. You answer the questionnaire; we write the copy in plain words and you approve it.
8. **Health, recovery or legal business?** We do these with extra care: no client information ever goes through Fiverr chat.
