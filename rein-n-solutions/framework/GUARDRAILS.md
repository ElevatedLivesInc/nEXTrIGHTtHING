# Guardrails — the lines that don't move

1. **Two entities, never blended.** For-profit service copy and nonprofit/donation copy never share a page or a post. (NRT: The Next Right Thing in Recovery vs. The Right Thing in Recovery, 501(c)(3).) The same rule applies to any client with a foundation.
2. **No client data in demos, portfolios, or Fiverr chat.** NRT holds treatment and sober-living records protected by 42 CFR Part 2 and HIPAA. Mission Control screenshots for sales are rebuilt with fictional data, every time.
3. **Ask NRT before naming the work publicly**, even generically ("a treatment and sober-living program in Utah").
4. **Google policy:** real business name only, no keyword stuffing, no fake or incentivized reviews, no review gating. Violations get listings suspended, which is the client's livelihood.
5. **Fiverr policy:** no off-platform contact or payment for Fiverr-originated buyers, no review requests tied to rewards, no fake orders.
6. **Recovery / sober-living niche gig:** no outcome or cure claims, no "insurance accepted" unless verified, no patient testimonials. Google Ads for addiction services require LegitScript certification; we don't sell ads in that niche. Utah sober-living homes must be licensed (Utah Office of Licensing). Confirm a buyer's license before building their site.
7. **Under-promise.** Never write a deliverable the SOP doesn't guarantee. "Live in 7 days," "dashboard numbers entered weekly," "Google verifies on its own timeline."
