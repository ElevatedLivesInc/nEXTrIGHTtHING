You have been wondering how to help

That is the whole reason this weekend exists. Not a gala. Not a pledge drive. A yard sale — the smallest, plainest, most ordinary door we could build.

Official drawing rules

2nd Annual End-of-Summer Yard Sale Prize Drawing — sponsored by The Right Thing in Recovery, a Utah 501(c)(3), EIN 93-4316358.

  1. No purchase or donation necessary to enter or win. A purchase or donation will not improve your chance of winning.
  2. Who may enter. Legal residents of Utah, 18 or older at time of entry. Employees, board members, contractors and current residents of The Right Thing in Recovery and The Next Right Thing in Recovery, and their immediate households, are not eligible.
  3. How to enter, free. Submit the online form on this page, or complete a paper entry slip at the sale during posted hours, or mail a 3x5 card with your name and one contact method to the address listed above. All three methods have equal weight.
  4. Entry period. Opens when this page is published and closes Monday, September 7, 2026 at the close of the sale.
  5. Limit. One entry per person. Duplicate entries are consolidated, not stacked.
  6. Prize. [[PRIZE]]. One winner. The prize is awarded as-is, has no cash equivalent, and is not transferable.
  7. Drawing and notification. The winner is selected at random from all valid entries on Monday, September 7, 2026, and contacted using the information provided within 3 days. If we cannot reach the winner within 14 days, an alternate is drawn.
  8. Taxes. The winner is responsible for any taxes owed on the prize.
  9. Your information. Entry information is used to run this drawing and is never sold. We contact you about anything else only if you checked the optional box.
  10. Sponsor. The Right Thing in Recovery, West Jordan, Utah. Void where prohibited.

Purchases at the yard sale are not tax-deductible, because you receive goods in exchange. Cash gifts and donated goods are deductible and receipted separately.
