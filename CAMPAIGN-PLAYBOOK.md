## Before anything ships — 2 blanks and 1 legal change

**Fill in:** the sale ADDRESS and the PRIZE. Both are marked `[[ADDRESS]]` / `[[PRIZE]]` in `yard-sale.html`
and flagged in a red banner at the top of the page. Delete the banner when done.

**The legal change:** the drawing is **free entry, no purchase necessary**. Utah bans paid raffles outright —
it's a constitutional prohibition with no charitable exemption (Utah Const. Art. VI §27; Utah Code 76-9-1401),
and a class B misdemeanor that reaches organizers, not just players. **"Spend $10 and get an entry" cannot ship.**
Every asset below is written for a free drawing. Have a Utah attorney read the posted rules before the sale opens.

This is not a downgrade. Free entry means people who *cannot come* can still enter — so the entry form becomes
your donor list. That list is worth more than the $10s you'd have collected.

## The through-line

> **"You've been wondering how to help. You just never knew how."**

That's the hook from the notes, and it's the emotional spine of every piece. The yard sale is the smallest
possible door into the organization. Three ways through it, in ascending commitment:

| Ask | Barrier | Where it lands |
|---|---|---|
| Enter the free drawing | Zero | Your donor list |
| Come buy something | A Saturday morning | Sale proceeds → nonprofit |
| Donate cash or goods | Real | Tax-deductible, receipted |

Never lead with the donation. Lead with the door.

## In-kind ask (T-6, Sat Aug 29)

Post this on its own. Specific asks outperform general ones by a wide margin — "we need a freezer" gets a
freezer, "we need your support" gets nothing.

> Two things we actually need right now: 🧊 **an upright freezer** and 🧺 **two washers.**
>
> Not glamorous. But laundry is a daily bottleneck across our houses, and freezer space is how we make food
> donations stretch.
>
> If you have one sitting unused — or can help us buy one — call **(385) 371-4515** before you load it up so we
> can confirm we can use it. Donated goods are tax-deductible and we'll issue your receipt with our EIN on the
> spot.
>
> We're also taking good, clean household items for the sale itself through Sunday.

**Fulfilment:** every in-kind gift gets an `RTIK-` code through your existing `/admin-issue` tool → donor gets a
verifiable receipt at `/in-kind-receipt`. That machinery already works — use it, don't hand-write receipts.

## Entity separation — the line that must not blur

| Money | Entity | Deductible? |
|---|---|---|
| Yard sale purchases | Proceeds → The Right Thing in Recovery | **No** — buyer receives goods |
| Cash gifts (Square) | The Right Thing in Recovery, 501(c)(3) | **Yes** — no goods provided |
| Donated goods (freezer, washers) | The Right Thing in Recovery | **Yes** — RTIK receipt, fair market value set by donor |
| Anything about treatment, intake, housing services | The Next Right Thing in Recovery | N/A — never in fundraising copy |

Every public asset carries the footer already used on last year's car wash page:
