Rent A Husband is a handyman crew working across the Salt Lake Valley. It was not started as a company that happens to hire people in recovery — it was started so people in recovery would have somewhere to work.

Neither tools nor experience is required. Crews go out two to four people at a time with a lead who knows the work, so nobody is sent somewhere alone to figure it out.

A background check is part of it, and a record does not automatically rule you out. That is rather the point — this exists because most places do rule you out.

Run by Koby. Licensed, insured, and an LLC, backed by a recovery center.
