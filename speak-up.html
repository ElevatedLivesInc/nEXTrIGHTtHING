Speak up.

If something is wrong — how you were treated, how your house is run, something you saw — tell us. You do not have to give your name.

Nothing here can be traced back to you unless you choose it. We do not record your name, your phone, or your device. Leave the contact box empty and the report is fully anonymous — we will read it and act on it, and we will have no way to know who sent it.

You can use this whether you are a client, a resident, a family member, or someone who works here. Speaking up is protected. Nobody is discharged, moved, or treated differently for raising a concern.
